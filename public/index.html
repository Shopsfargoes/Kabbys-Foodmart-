Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add items by kg, check out, and pay securely with Paystack. We confirm your order by email and phone.

Pickup or delivery?

Both. Pickup is free. For delivery, choose your area at checkout and the fee is added automatically.

Do you deliver to other cities?

Yes, for selected items and cities. The fee depends on the city and the weight of your order.

Can I place a custom or bulk order?

Yes. Send a request on the custom order page and we will reply with a quote.

How can I pay?

Payments are handled securely by Paystack. We never see your card details.
