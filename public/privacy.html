Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kabby’s Foodmart (“we”, “us”) is a food store in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this website, why, and the choices you have. We handle your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • Your delivery address, if you choose delivery.
  • What you order, your order notes, and any custom order request you send.
  • Your account email, created automatically when you place your first order so you can track it.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by Paystack.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and confirm your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To reply to custom and bulk order requests.
  • To let you view and track your orders in your account.
  • To keep our records, prevent fraud and protect the website.

Who we share it with

Only the people and services needed to run your order:

  • Paystack, to process your payment.
  • Our delivery rider or courier, who receives your name, phone number and delivery address to deliver your order.
  • Our technology providers, for website hosting, our database and sending emails.

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for our business and legal records. You can ask us to delete your account and personal details, except where we must keep records.

Cookies and local storage

We store your shopping cart in your browser so it is still there when you come back. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose you did not agree to. To do so, contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Access to customer information is limited to authorised staff, and our systems use encryption and access controls. No online service can be perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
